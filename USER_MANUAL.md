## 1. Introduction & Key Capabilities

**Kru Fitness** is an all-in-one cloud management platform designed specifically for gyms, fitness studios, crossfit boxes, and personal trainers. It replaces traditional paper registers, messy Excel sheets, and complex software with a fast, modern, mobile-friendly interface.

### 🌟 Top Features at a Glance:
- **Zero Installation Hassle:** Runs seamlessly on Android, iOS, Windows, Mac, and Tablets.
- **Smart Member Profiles:** Photo, contact, emergency details, blood group, medical notes, and membership history.
- **Instant WhatsApp Integration:** Send welcome messages, due reminders, and payment receipts directly to members' WhatsApp numbers without saving contacts.
- **Automated Due Alerts:** Never lose revenue; automatically identifies members whose plan expires in the next 3, 5, or 7 days.
- **Dynamic UPI QR Code:** Generates a custom UPI QR code for the exact fee amount, enabling members to scan and pay via GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, or BHIM.
- **Fast Attendance:** 1-second check-in system with optional QR code scanning.
- **Multi-Branch Support:** Manage multiple gym locations from a single owner account.

## 2. How to Access & Install as Mobile App

Kru Fitness is built as a **Progressive Web App (PWA)**. You and your gym members do not need to download heavy files from app stores. It works directly from the web link and can be installed as a full-screen app.

**Live Application Link:**  
👉 `https://kru-fitness-v2.vercel.app/`

---

### <a id="android-install"></a>📱 Android Phone Installation (Chrome)
1. Open Google Chrome on your Android mobile.
2. Visit `https://kru-fitness-v2.vercel.app/`.
3. Tap the **three dots (`⋮`)** in the top-right corner of Chrome.
4. Select **"Install app"** or **"Add to Home screen"**.
5. Tap **Add / Install**.
6. **Done!** The **Kru Fitness** icon will appear on your phone's home screen. When opened, it runs in fullscreen mode without any browser address bar.

---

### <a id="ios-install"></a>🍏 iPhone / iPad Installation (Safari)
1. Open **Safari** browser on your iPhone or iPad.
2. Visit `https://kru-fitness-v2.vercel.app/`.
3. Tap the **Share icon** (the square with an arrow pointing up at the bottom).
4. Scroll down and tap **"Add to Home Screen"**.
5. Tap **Add** in the top-right corner.
6. The Kru Fitness app icon is now on your iOS home screen!

---

### <a id="desktop-install"></a>💻 Desktop / Reception Counter PC (Chrome / Edge)
1. Open **Google Chrome** or **Microsoft Edge** on your reception computer.
2. Visit `https://kru-fitness-v2.vercel.app/`.
3. Look at the right side of the address bar: click the small **Monitor / Install icon** (`💻`).
4. Click **Install**.
5. Kru Fitness will open as a dedicated desktop window. You can pin it to your Windows Taskbar or Mac Dock for instant 1-click access.

## 3. Sign Up & Login Guide

Kru Fitness supports two quick, secure login methods:

### <a id="email-otp"></a>Method A: 6-Digit Email OTP / Magic Link
1. On the login screen, choose **"6-Digit Email OTP"**.
2. Enter your **Name** (if signing up for the first time) and **Email Address**.
3. Click **"Send 6-Digit OTP to Register / Sign In"**.
4. Check your email inbox (or Spam/Updates folder):
   - You can either **click the "Sign In" link** in the email for instant one-click login.
   - Or **type the 6-digit verification code** on the screen.
5. You are instantly logged in!

### <a id="password-login"></a>Method B: Email & Password
1. Click the **"🔑 Password"** tab.
2. Enter your Email and Password.
3. Click **"Sign In"** (or switch to **Create Account** tab if registering for the first time).
4. Instant login without needing to open your email!

## 4. First-Time Gym Setup

When you log in for the first time, the **Gym Setup Wizard** will appear:

### <a id="gym-profile"></a>1. Gym Profile Details
- **Gym Name:** e.g., *Kru Fitness Club & Spa*
- **Branch / Location:** e.g., *Main Branch, Civil Lines*
- **Phone Number:** Official gym contact number for member communication.
- **Address & City:** Printed on invoices and member receipts.

### <a id="upi-setup"></a>2. UPI ID Setup (Crucial for Fees)
- Enter your Gym's official UPI ID (e.g., `gymname@okaxis`, `9876543210@paytm`).
- Whenever you collect fees from a member, Kru Fitness generates a dynamic QR code with this UPI ID and the exact fee amount.

### <a id="multi-branch"></a>3. Multi-Branch Support
- If you own multiple gyms or branches, you can click on the Gym Switcher dropdown in the top-left corner of the dashboard and select **"+ Add New Branch"**.
- Switch between branches anytime without logging out.

## 5. Managing Membership Plans

Before adding members, configure your gym's membership packages:

1. Navigate to **Settings ➔ Membership Plans**.
2. Click **"+ Add Plan"**.
3. Fill in the plan details:
   - **Plan Name:** e.g., *Monthly Standard, Quarterly Cardio+Weight, Annual VIP*
   - **Duration:** 1 Month, 3 Months, 6 Months, 12 Months, or Custom Days.
   - **Fee Amount:** Standard fee (e.g., ₹1,500, ₹4,000, ₹12,000).
   - **Description:** Features included (e.g., Locker, Steam Bath, Personal Diet Chart).
4. Click **Save Plan**.

## 6. Member Management

### <a id="add-member"></a>Adding a New Member
1. Click on the **"Members"** tab from the left sidebar (or bottom bar on mobile).
2. Click the **"+ Add New Member"** button.
3. Enter Member Details:
   - **Full Name** & **Mobile Number** (Essential for WhatsApp alerts).
   - **Gender & Date of Birth**.
   - **Emergency Contact** (Name & Phone).
   - **Batch:** Morning (6 AM - 10 AM) / Evening (4 PM - 9 PM) / General.
   - **Select Plan:** Choose one of your pre-configured plans.
   - **Start Date:** Defaults to today.
4. **Initial Payment:**
   - Choose whether the member is paying now or paying later.
   - If paying now, select Payment Mode: **UPI / Cash / Card / Net Banking**.
   - Enter amount received (supports partial payment).
5. Click **"Register Member"**.
6. The member profile is created, the fee receipt is generated, and you can send a welcome message on WhatsApp with one click!

### <a id="member-profile"></a>Member Profile Overview
Click on any member in the list to open their **Digital File**:
- Membership status: `Active` (Green), `Expiring Soon` (Yellow), `Expired` (Red), `On Hold` (Blue).
- Complete payment history and pending balance.
- Total days attended this month.
- Quick action buttons: **Collect Fee**, **Renew Plan**, **Hold Membership**, **WhatsApp Chat**.

## 7. Payments & Digital Receipts

### <a id="collect-fees"></a>Collecting Fees
1. Go to the **"Payments"** tab or click **"Collect Fee"** on any member's card.
2. Select or search the Member.
3. The pending due amount or plan amount is automatically filled.
4. Choose Payment Mode:
   - **UPI / QR Code:** A dynamic QR code appears on the screen. The member scans it on their phone (GPay/PhonePe/Paytm) to pay directly into your account!
   - **Cash:** For physical cash received at the counter.
   - **Card / POS:** For debit/credit card swipe machines.
   - **Bank Transfer / Net Banking:** For direct NEFT/IMPS transfers.
5. Click **"Record Payment"**.

### <a id="receipts"></a>Digital Invoices & WhatsApp Sharing
- Every recorded payment generates a professional, GST-compliant digital receipt.
- Click **"Print Receipt"** for thermal or A4 printing.
- Click **"Send on WhatsApp"**: It opens WhatsApp Web or the WhatsApp App with a formatted invoice message ready to send with one click!

## 8. Renewals & Membership Hold (Pause)

### <a id="renew-plan"></a>Renewing a Plan
When a member's plan expires:
1. Open the **"Renewals"** tab or member profile.
2. Click **"Renew Membership"**.
3. Choose the renewal plan (existing or upgrade to a new package).
4. Set the new start date (automatically starts from the previous end date or today).
5. Record payment and click **"Confirm Renewal"**.

### <a id="hold-membership"></a>Membership Hold / Pause (Chhutti / Illness)
If a member goes out of town, has exams, or gets injured:
1. Open the member's profile.
2. Click **"Hold Membership"**.
3. Enter the number of hold days (e.g., 15 days) and the reason.
4. Kru Fitness automatically freezes their expiry date and extends their end date forward by the exact number of days!

## 9. Daily Attendance & QR Check-in System

Keep track of member footfall and identify inactive members:

### <a id="quick-checkin"></a>1. 1-Second Manual Check-in

## 11. Staff & Role Permissions

Control what your team can see and do:
- **Owner (Super Admin):** Full access to revenue, settings, branch deletion, and member management.
- **Manager:** Can add members, collect fees, manage attendance, and view day-to-day reports.
- **Receptionist / Front Desk:** Can mark attendance, register new members, and collect fees. Revenue analytics and gym settings are hidden.
- **Trainer:** Can view member lists, batches, and mark attendance. Financial data is hidden.

## 12. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) & Troubleshooting

#### Q1: Does Kru Fitness require any special hardware or machine?
**No.** Any smartphone (Android/iPhone), tablet, laptop, or desktop computer with internet access is completely sufficient.

#### Q2: Can multiple staff members use the software at the same time?
**Yes.** The receptionist can mark attendance at the desk while the gym owner views financial reports on their phone simultaneously. All data updates in real-time.

#### Q3: What happens if our reception internet is temporarily down?
Kru Fitness caches session data so you can view member phone numbers even during brief internet drops. When internet reconnects, all pending syncs are completed.

#### Q4: How do I backup my gym data?
Your data is securely stored in Google Cloud and Supabase PostgreSQL with automated daily backups. You can also export your member and payment records to an Excel file at any time from **Reports ➔ Export**.

#### Q5: Can I send WhatsApp reminders without saving the member's phone number?
**Yes.** Kru Fitness uses direct WhatsApp API links (`https://wa.me/...`). Clicking the WhatsApp button automatically opens the chat with the pre-written receipt or reminder without requiring you to save their contact.
